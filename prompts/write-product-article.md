## Article Structure

### 1. Introduction (150-200 words)

- Hook the reader with a relatable pain point or scenario
- State what you'll cover
- Include primary keyword naturally

### 2. Quick Picks Table

Create a comparison table at the top:

```markdown
| Product     | Best For     | Price Range | Rating     |
| ----------- | ------------ | ----------- | ---------- |
| [Product 1] | Best Overall | $$$         | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| [Product 2] | Budget Pick  | $           | ⭐⭐⭐⭐   |
```

### 3. Individual Product Reviews

For each product:

- **H2:** Product name
- **Opening:** Why we recommend it (1-2 sentences)
- **Key Features:** 3-5 bullet points
- **Pros/Cons:** Honest assessment
- **Who It's For:** Ideal user profile
- **Verdict:** Clear recommendation

### 4. Buying Guide (optional)

- What to look for when buying
- Key features to consider
- Common mistakes to avoid

### 5. Conclusion

- Summarize top picks
- Final recommendation
- Call to action

### 6. Affiliate Disclosure

Include this at the bottom:

> **Affiliate Disclosure:** Some links in this article are affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no extra cost to you.

---

## Writing Guidelines

1. **Be Genuine:** Write like you've actually used these products
2. **Be Specific:** Include specific features, dimensions, prices where known
3. **Be Balanced:** Include cons, not just pros
4. **Be Helpful:** Focus on helping the reader make a decision
5. **Avoid Hype:** Don't use "amazing", "incredible", "game-changer"
6. **Use Active Voice:** "The backpack holds 40L" not "40L is held by the backpack"

## Example Product Section

```markdown
## Osprey Farpoint 40

The Farpoint 40 is our top pick for most travelers. It's the perfect size for carry-on, opens like a suitcase, and has Osprey's legendary durability.

**Key Features:**

- 40L capacity (maximum carry-on size)
- Clamshell opening for easy packing
- Stowable shoulder straps and hip belt
- Padded laptop sleeve up to 15"
- Lockable zippers

**Pros:**

- Excellent build quality and warranty
- Comfortable for all-day wear
- Versatile for both travel and hiking

**Cons:**

- Limited external pockets
- No water bottle pocket
- Premium price point

**Who It's For:** Frequent flyers who want a durable, versatile pack that works for both airports and day hikes.

**Verdict:** If you travel more than a few times a year, the Farpoint 40 is worth the investment.
```
